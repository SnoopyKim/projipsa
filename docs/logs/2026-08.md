## [2026-08-13] decision | A rule now has to earn its place

- reviewed: `mattpocock/skills` as the stated benchmark; 35 skills, `SKILL.md`
  median about 3.5 KB, and no validation script anywhere under `skills/`
- measured: this plugin ships about 99 KB of prose against 27 KB of script,
  restates `logs/YYYY-MM.md` and `append-only` in five files each, and its
  validator enforces roughly 33 hard errors
- found: the doubt was aimed at the scripts, but the weight is in the prose,
  and most of that prose exists to explain the rules
- decided: grade a rule by what a future reader loses when it breaks — wrong
  conclusion is an error, slowed but recovering is a warning, unaffected is not
  a rule; the two rows match the validator's existing channels
- changed: `memory-contract.md` opens with the criterion, and the pointer in
  `shared/projipsa.md` now names rule weighing as a reason to read it; the
  criterion is stated once and nowhere restated
- graded for removal, not yet removed: `related`, three of the five required
  files, the required decision page, and required `type`; `status` and
  `confidence` become defaults when absent; the confirmed-evidence chain drops
  to a warning
- corrected: the Projipsa adoption decision reads as ceremony but carries
  initialization idempotency, so it is the deliberate exception rather than a
  removal candidate
- decision: wiki/decisions/2026-08-13-rules-must-earn-their-place.md
- validation: package validator, 46 tests on Python 3.12 and 3.9.6, the memory
  validator over 9 maintained pages, and `claude plugin validate
  ./plugins/projipsa --strict` pass
- warning: current state crossed the size budget during this entry's own work
  and now reports 12,208 characters. The 0.3.2 release evidence it duplicated
  from the chronology was evicted, which was not enough; the rest of the page is
  still-true content that the current eviction rule does not reach, which is the
  case the eviction predicate is meant to fix
- not done: no rule was actually removed, no version bump, and no release
